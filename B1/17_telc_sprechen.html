The exam (Mündlicher Ausdruck)
  • You take it with a partner. It lasts about 15 minutes. Before it you have 20 minutes to prepare.
  • Teil 1: you get to know each other (name, country, family, work, hobbies, why you learn German). Ask questions too.
  • Teil 2: each of you gets a card with one person's opinion. You present your card, say your opinion and ask your partner.
  • Teil 3: you both get the same card (for example a party or a trip) and plan together.
