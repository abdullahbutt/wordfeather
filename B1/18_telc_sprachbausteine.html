telc B1 Sprachbausteine: Prüfungen und Grammatik

Grammar is part of the exam: the Sprachbausteine exams of the book, and the 5-minute grammar refresher with a practice mode.

Sprachbausteine: the grammar part of the exam
  • Teil 1: a text with 10 gaps (21–30), you choose a, b or c. Teil 2: a text with 10 gaps (31–40), you choose from a list of 15 words (a–o), each word only once.
  • The Sprachbausteine share the 90 minutes with Lesen (see the order tip on 15 telc Lesen).
  • Grammar also carries Schreiben and Sprechen: after dass and weil the verb goes at the end.

The texts are the 18 Sprachbausteine of the book "Prüfungs Buch Petra" (Sonja and Rita: scanned pages, typed from the images). The book's answer keys have a few mistakes; where the printed letter does not fit its word, the page says so.

Cheat sheet: how to find the right word (Teil 1 and Teil 2)

1. Four steps for every gap

  1. Read the whole text once (use Show the English after your own try) so you know what the text is about.
  2. Look at the words next to the gap and decide the type of word that is missing: verb, article or pronoun, preposition, connector or small word.
  3. Decide the form: which person or tense (verb), which case and gender (article, adjective, pronoun), which case (preposition).
  4. Teil 2: only now look at the list. Do the sure gaps first (verbs, mir/mich, articles), the connector words last. Every word is used once, so the crossed-out words help you.

3. Endings (Teil 1)

  • Only the masculine changes from Nominativ to Akkusativ (der → den, ein → einen).
  • Dativ: m and n end in -m, f ends in -r, plural ends in -n (and the noun gets an extra n: mit den Kindern).
  • Adjective after der / die / das: -e in the Nominativ (all genders) and in the Akkusativ feminine and neuter; -en everywhere else. Example: mit freundlichen Grüßen (Dativ plural → -en).
  • welcher / welche / welches / welchen follow the same endings as der / die / das / den: mit welchem Zug? welchen Zug nimmst du?

5. Verb forms (Teil 1)

  • Modal verb + infinitive at the end: Ich muss heute arbeiten. Not "arbeite".
  • Perfekt: haben / sein in position 2, Partizip II at the end: Ich habe ihn gefunden. Ich bin gefahren.
  • Präteritum of modals: konnte, wollte, musste, durfte, sollte (after als and weil in a past story).
  • Imperative: du: Schreib! / ihr: Schreibt! / Sie: Schreiben Sie! Ask who is addressed.
  • Subject and verb must match: er zeigt, sie zeigen. Find the subject first.

6. Small words that look alike

  • mich / mir: mich = Akkusativ (ich freue mich, er sieht mich); mir = Dativ (er hilft mir, es gefällt mir).
  • dass / das: dass opens a clause (verb at the end); das is an article or pronoun.
  • wann / wenn / als: wann = question; wenn = if, whenever; als = one single time in the past.
  • ob / wenn: ob = whether (yes/no question inside a sentence): Ich weiß nicht, ob er kommt.
  • statt / anstatt, trotz / trotzdem: statt and trotz are prepositions (+ noun); trotzdem is an adverb (alone in the sentence).
  • damit: a connector (so that, verb at the end) and a da-word (with it), depending on the sentence.

8. Wish forms: a wish, not a fact

  • hatte = had (past fact), hätte = would have (wish). war = was, wäre = would be. Ich hatte Zeit. (fact) · Ich hätte gern Zeit. (I would like to have time.)
  • konnte → könnte (could), musste → müsste (would have to).
  • wurde / wurden = past of werden (became, was done). würde / würden = would: a wish or a polite request, with an infinitive at the end.
  • Person: würde goes with ich, er, sie. würden goes with wir, Sie, sie (they).

9. Teil 2: the 10-minute plan

  1. 1 minute: sort the word box with the letters C-P-P-V-A-A: Connectors, Prepositions, Pronouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs. Exactly 5 words stay unused.
  2. 2 minutes: the sure gaps (fixed pairs, verb-at-the-end gaps).
  3. 5 minutes: the rest with the neighbour tricks below.
  4. 1 minute: cross out the used words; exactly 5 must stay. 1 minute spare.

Neighbour tricks: look only 2 words left and right of the gap.

  1. Article + gap + noun (die __ Zusendung) → an adjective.
  2. Gap + plural noun (noch __ Fragen) → einige, ein paar, beiden, keine.
  3. Gap + noun with no article → a preposition (zweimal pro Woche).
  4. Gap at the start, “?” at the end, a base verb last → a modal verb.
  5. After a comma, the verb at the end → a connector. Yes/no question → ob (Ich möchte wissen, ob man im Voraus bezahlen muss.). “If” or “when” → wenn (Ich freue mich, wenn du kommst.). A question word → wann, wo, welche (Sagen Sie mir bitte, wann der Kurs beginnt.).
  6. Gap between the verb and the rest → a small word (möchte ich mich schon jetzt vorbereiten).

10. Teil 1: the 5 rules (End · Article · Two · Three · Person)

  • End: the verb is at the end of the clause → a connector (dass, weil, wenn, ob).
  • Article: a noun comes after the gap → an article or a preposition. Decide the case first (section 3).
  • Two: hat / ist is already there → the past form (gemacht). A modal or würde is there → the base verb at the end (machen).
  • Three: weil, obwohl, dass → the verb goes to the end. deshalb, trotzdem, dann → the verb comes next. und, aber, oder, denn, sondern → nothing changes.
  • Person: the verb ending must fit the person (er zeigt, sie zeigen).
  • Stuck for 30 seconds? Skip it and come back. Never leave a gap empty: cross out 2 options and guess.

11. Don't know the article? 5 tricks

  1. Find the same noun elsewhere in the text (im Haus → Haus is der or das, not die).
  2. Endings: -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät → die · -chen, -lein → das.
  3. A long word: the last part decides (Hauptbahnhof → der Bahnhof → der).
  4. Plural → die, or den + -n in the Dativ (mit den Kindern).
  5. Decide the case first, then cross out (mit __ Freund → mit = Dativ → dem). A pronoun later helps: er → a der-word, sie → a die-word, es → a das-word.

Relative pronouns (der, die, das, den, dem, denen): they stand right after a comma, point back to the noun before the comma, and send the verb of their part to the end. Ask: 1. Which noun does it point to? (gender or plural) 2. What does it do in its own part? (subject, object, after a preposition: the case) 3. Pick the form. They are the same as the articles (der/die/das), except the Dativ plural: denen. Nominativ · Akkusativ · Dativ: m der · den · dem, f die · die · der, n das · das · dem, plural die · die · denen.

12. Fixed pairs worth learning

  • zwar … aber = it is true … but · nicht nur … sondern auch = not only … but also · sowohl … als auch = both … and
  • so … wie möglich = as … as possible · am liebsten = most of all · im Voraus = in advance · pro Woche = per week
  • bitten um = to ask for · Antwort auf = answer to · interessiert an = interested in · geeignet für = suitable for · sich interessieren für · sich gewöhnen an = to get used to
